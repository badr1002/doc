Setup checklist

Everything required before going live at the event. Most items take under a minute to verify.

Required

The AI Engine

Powered by Google Gemini

The AI transformation is the highlight — and the experience is built to keep going even when AI isn't available.

🤖 How the AI works
When a guest completes the booth, their photo is sent to Google Gemini along with the context from their quiz answers and their chosen Curl Energy. Gemini returns a beautifully styled version of their image. The final Polaroid is then assembled on the device with full Sunsilk branding — no data leaves the kiosk.
1
Guest completes quiz + booth photo
2
Photo + mood context sent to Gemini API
3
Gemini returns AI-styled curl image
4
Polaroid assembled locally with branding
5
Guest scans QR to save their keepsake
⚠️ If AI is unavailable
The app never crashes. It gives guests 3 clear options to keep the session moving:
🔄 Try again
📷 Use original photo
↩️ Retake photo
✅ Fix for organizers
Enable a Google One AI Premium subscription, confirm the Gemini API project has billing active, and verify the API key in the app's .env file has image-generation access.
Best Practices

Tips for a great demo day

Small setup decisions that make the experience feel polished and professional.

💡
Use good, even lighting on the guest's face and hair — this directly improves the AI transformation quality.
👤
Frame head & shoulders in the camera view. Make sure curls are clearly visible for the best styling result.
📱
Always use landscape orientation on the iPad. This is how the app's layout is designed to be seen.
🔇
Silence notifications and disable screen auto-lock before the event starts to prevent interruptions.
🔁
Always tap DONE after each guest. This clears the session so the next guest starts completely fresh.
🌐
Test your internet connection before the event. The AI step needs a stable connection to run smoothly.
Troubleshooting

If something goes wrong

Quick fixes for every common issue. Keep this page open during the event.

⏳ Splash screen never ends
Force-quit the app and reopen it. Check that the device has enough available storage.
🚫 Camera is blocked
Go to Settings → Privacy → Camera and allow access for the app. Then reopen the booth.
✨ AI error or stuck on processing
Tap Use Original Photo to keep the session moving. Check Gemini billing and API key status after the event.
📲 QR code won't scan
Wait for the share screen to fully load. Try saving from a second device if the primary is slow.
🧴 Guest received the wrong routine
Restart from the welcome screen and ensure all 3 quiz questions are answered before proceeding.
